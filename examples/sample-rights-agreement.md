# MEDIA RIGHTS AGREEMENT (SAMPLE — SYNTHETIC, NOT A REAL CONTRACT)

This Media Rights Agreement (the "Agreement") is entered into by and between Riverside Sports, LLC
("Team") and Northstar Networks, Inc. ("Network").

## Section 1. Term

The term of this Agreement is seven (7) years, commencing with the 2024-25 season and continuing
through the final game of the 2030-31 season, unless terminated sooner in accordance with this
Agreement (the "Term").

## Section 2. Grant of Rights; Exclusivity

The Team hereby grants to the Network the exclusive right to produce, telecast, and distribute the
Team's games throughout the Territory during the Term. Such rights are exclusive and no other party
may telecast the games within the Territory.

## Section 3. Most Favored Nation

The Network shall provide the Team with most-favored-nation treatment such that if the Network grants
any other team in the League more favorable per-game rights fees, the Network shall promptly extend
those more favorable terms to the Team.

## Section 4. Termination for Convenience

Either party may terminate this Agreement for convenience upon one hundred eighty (180) days' prior
written notice to the other party.

## Section 5. Governing Law

This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Illinois,
without reference to its conflicts of law principles.
